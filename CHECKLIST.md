# Open Dredmor — Implementation Checklist

This checklist tracks all functionality needed to go from the current Godot 4 skeleton to a fully playable clean-room reimplementation of *Dungeons of Dredmor* using the user's local Dredmor installation assets.

The checklist is split into ordered chunks under `checklist/`:

1. [01 — Bootstrap, Asset Loading & Database](checklist/01_bootstrap_and_assets.md)
2. [02 — Menus, Settings & Display](checklist/02_menus_settings_and_display.md)
3. [03 — Dungeon Generation, Tilesets & Room Features](checklist/03_dungeon_generation_and_rooms.md)
4. [04 — Turn-Based Loop, Commands, Pathfinding & FOV](checklist/04_turn_loop_commands_and_movement.md)
5. [05 — Character, Skills, Combat & Monsters](checklist/05_character_skills_and_combat.md)
6. [06 — Items, Inventory, Loot & Crafting](checklist/06_items_inventory_and_crafting.md)
7. [07 — In-Game HUD & UX](checklist/07_hud_and_ux.md)
8. [08 — Save / Load & Persistence](checklist/08_save_load_and_persistence.md)
